## Anthony Val C. Camposano

**Postdoctoral researcher, Linköping University, Sweden**

I work in computational physics and molecular simulation. My current research uses machine learning to model biopolymer self-assembly. Previously, I developed reactive models for water and silica and worked on quantum transport simulations.

[Research overview]({% post_url others/about_me/2025-03-04-my-research %}) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com.ph/citations?user=cOoW4-4AAAAJ&hl=en) · [GitHub](https://github.com/antble)

### Research interests

Molecular dynamics and Monte Carlo simulation; machine learning for molecular systems; reactive force fields; scientific computing.

### Research and education

- **Postdoctoral research**, Linköping University (April 2026–present). Machine learning models of biopolymer self-assembly.
- **PhD in Physics**, University of Oslo (October 2021–December 2025). Reactive silica–water models using the Vashishta potential. Thesis: [*Parameterisation of a Reactive Silica-Water Model using the Vashishta Functional Form*](https://nva.sikt.no/registration/0199aa1ca0ce-8a60fe61-b8df-4f9e-aa45-fcfba327e9c3).
- **MS in Physics**, University of San Carlos (June 2017–July 2020). Quantum transport in nanostructures using the lattice Weyl-Wigner formalism. Thesis: *1D Density Functional Theory + Lattice Weyl-Wigner Function Quantum Transport Modelling*.
