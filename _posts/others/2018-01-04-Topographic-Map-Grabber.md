<p>
The 1:250,000 scale map is divided into smaller tiles at a scale of 1:50,000. Clicking each tile was frustrating due to the low resolution, so I decided to let a <a href="https://github.com/sinbad989/Python_Programming/tree/master/TopoMapGrabber">Python script</a> do it for me. I used it to download all the 1:50,000 resolution maps.
