Keep Writing. Anything.

"The act of composition disciplines the mind; writing is one way to go about thinking, and the practice and habit of writing not only drain the mind, but supply it too." Strunk and White, The Elements of Style, 3rd ed., p.70

This blog has three goals in mind:

  1. To keep writing.
    • What's the most interesting thing I've learned this week?
  2. To keep learning.
    • What topic or idea am I curious about?
  3. To keep sharing.
    • What topic or idea I've written about needs an update?
This blog is a working archive: thoughts, ideas, notes. Dates indicate the last time a post was modified. The 'beton brut' aesthetic is deliberate. It is raw, unfinished, and intentionally left as a work in progress.

Mainly divided into 3 main categories: Physics, Programming, and Math. And then of course, other stuff ...
